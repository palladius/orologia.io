How BCD works: Each digit of the time is coded separately in binary! Active glowing lights represent binary "1" and inactive represent "0". Add up the values of the glowing lights in each column to get the decimal digit!

💡 Time-Reading Guide

The Clock Hands (Asticelle)

The short Blue hand shows the Hour. The long Pink hand shows the Minutes.

Quarter Sub-Ticks (Guides)

Struggling to know where the hour hand should point? Look closely at the small dots between each hour number on our clock face:

  • 🔵 Blue Dot: Quarter past (:15). Hour hand is 1/4 of the way to the next hour.
  • 🟡 Yellow Dot: Half past (:30). Hour hand is halfway to the next hour.
  • 🔴 Pink Dot: Quarter to (:45). Hour hand is 3/4 of the way to the next hour.

This helps Sebi and Ale see why the hour hand isn't pointing directly at a number when it's e.g., 4:45!

For Alessandro (Medium/Hard)

Remember that PM hours convert to 24-hour time! Subtract 12 from hours greater than 12 to find the position of the short hand on the clock face.
